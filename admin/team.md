# Team Hi5 ✋

## Team Brand

Our team, **Hi5**, is built around collaboration and strong team energy. The name comes from a high-five, which represents teamwork, support, and shared success. We wanted something simple and recognizable that reflects how we work together.

Our branding is clean and consistent. We use a high-five hand as our main visual, along with bold and simple text. Our color palette focuses on dark tones with blue accents to keep everything sharp and easy to recognize. Our tagline, **“Built Different,”** reflects how we approach our work; steady, reliable, and a little more creative when needed.

We use these elements across our presentations, documents, and video so our team identity stays consistent throughout the project. Our Slack workspace icon follows the same style and includes our team number.

## Team Values

- We communicate clearly and keep everyone updated
- We take responsibility for our work
- We stay consistent instead of rushing last minute
- We support each other when someone is stuck
- We focus on getting things done properly

## Team Culture

We keep things structured but not overly strict. Everyone is expected to contribute and stay on track, but we also make sure the environment is comfortable so people can share ideas and ask for help.

Our goal is to stay organized, build something solid, and represent our team well throughout the project.
